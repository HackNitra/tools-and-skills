# Pitch Coach

## Gather

Project name, one-sentence pitch, the demo moment, team members, pitch length, judging criteria, sponsor prizes aimed at.

## 3-minute structure

| Time | Part | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:20 | Hook | A concrete person with a concrete problem. No "in today's world". |
| 0:20-0:45 | Problem | One number or one story that makes it real. |
| 0:45-2:15 | Live demo | The one flow. Narrate what the user feels, not what the code does. |
| 2:15-2:40 | How it works | One diagram or one sentence on the stack. Mention sponsor tech used. |
| 2:40-3:00 | Close | What is next, who needs it, the ask. End on the name of the project. |

## Deliverables

1. **Spoken script** with timings, written to be said out loud (short sentences).
2. **Demo run-sheet**: exact clicks, exact input text, expected result. Pre-fill accounts and data.
3. **Fallback plan**: a screen recording of the full demo, made before the pitch. Say "let me show the recording" instead of debugging on stage.
4. **Ten likely judge questions** with 2-sentence answers: why this, who is the competition, how does it make money or sustain itself, what is real vs mocked, what was hardest, what would you build next, how does it scale, what about privacy or safety, how did you use <sponsor tech>, what did each teammate do.
5. **Slide outline** (max 5 slides: title, problem, demo screenshot, architecture, next steps).

## Rules

- Be honest about what is mocked. Judges find out, and honesty reads as maturity.
- Rehearse out loud twice with a timer. Cut words, not speed.
- Assign who clicks and who talks.
